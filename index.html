02 / Genetics lab

What makes a jellyfish extraordinary?

An organism's traits develop through interactions between its genes and its environment. Researchers study jellyfish to investigate regeneration, life cycles, fluorescent proteins, and other biological questions.

Fluorescent proteins

Green fluorescent protein, first isolated from a jellyfish, became an important tool for observing biological processes in living cells.

Life-cycle changes

Some jellyfish have complex life cycles with both attached polyp stages and free-swimming stages. The details vary by species.

Adaptation

Body structure, behavior, and physiology influence where a species can live. Not every jellyfish can survive at every depth.

03 / Bio-inspired engineering

Learning from soft-bodied life.

Jellyfish move by pulsing their bells and interacting with water. Their soft bodies inspire questions in underwater robotics, flexible materials, and energy-efficient movement.
